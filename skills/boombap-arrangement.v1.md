# Boom bap / 90s hip hop

Zero-based beats per bar: kick 0, 1.75, 2.5 (vary), snare 1.0 and 3.0 ALWAYS (vel 0.9+), hats 8ths w/ swing,
open hat on 3.5 sometimes. Sample-flavored melody: piano/epiano 2-bar loop phrase, dusty (fx_saturate drive 6
tone 3000 + fx_reverb mix 0.15). keys_dark or epiano kit for that MPC feel. Keep it LOOPY: 2-bar melodic loop
that repeats with tiny variations beats structure changes.
