# Drum & bass / jungle

Two-bar break pattern with variations: kick 0, 2.5 / snare 1.0, 3.0 / ghosts at 1.75, 3.25, 3.75 rotating.
Reese bass: 2-4 bar notes, dark minor walk (root, b6, b7, 5), fx_saturate drive 8 for growl, NO widen (keep low mono).
Pads: strings/warmpad long swells, heavy reverb (mix 0.35 size 5). Lead: pluck2 or fmbell fast arpeggio bursts
between snare hits. Intro 8 bars pads+filtered break, DROP = full bass at bar 9.
