# House / techno

Kick: kick_punch (not 808 — tight, punchy) every beat, vel steady 0.85-0.95. Closed hats 16ths quiet (0.35-0.5)
under offbeat opens. Bass: sub_clean or pluckbass, offbeat 8ths (0.5, 1.5...) root notes, pumped. Chords: warmpad
or epiano stabs on offbeats (house piano!), fx_pump + fx_reverb 0.25. Arp/lead sparse. Energy via LAYER COUNT
across 8-bar blocks: bars 1-8 drums+bass, 9-16 +chords, 17-24 +lead. Techno variant: 126-132, darker (keys_dark),
less chords, more filtered pad movement (pad_motion), tighter reverb.
