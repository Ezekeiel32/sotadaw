# Lofi hip hop

Drums soft: kick 0 / 2.6-ish (loose), snare 1.0+0.04, 3.0+0.03 (behind the beat!), hats 8ths vel 0.3-0.5 with
holes. Chords: 2-4 minor-7th chords per 2 bars on epiano (e.g. Am7-Dm7-Gm7-C7 shapes), arpeggiate some. Bass:
sub_clean quarter/half notes following roots, quiet (-10). Texture: pad_motion very low (-16) for air. Space:
fx_reverb mix 0.25 size 2.5 on melody, fx_delay time=8th feedback 0.3 mix 0.15 on epiano. Keep it SMALL and warm:
masterGainDb -2, LUFS target -16 to -14, nothing aggressive.
