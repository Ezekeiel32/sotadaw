# DJ blending (v4 — blends AND cuts, the full event grammar)

The DAW is the DJ. `dj_blend(deck_a, deck_b, song)` builds one transition;
`dj_set(tracks[], song)` builds a WHOLE SET: N warped decks on a traveling grid,
phrase-aligned transitions, bass swaps — and it PICKS THE TRANSITION STYLE per
hop: beat-matched staggered blend within warp distance, ECHO-OUT CUT beyond 16%
(a funk 104 deck can hand over to techno 132 — genre-traveling events are one
tool call). Everything is one song.json: every move is visible automation you
(or the user) can drag afterwards.

## The blend recipe (what dj_blend writes, so you can edit or hand-roll it)
- Decks: one track per song, `source {"kind":"audio"}`, `audioClips[{file, startBeat, sourceBpm}]`,
  `fx[0] = fx_djfilter (knob 0, res 1.1)`.
- Grid: song bpm = deck A's bpm by default. Deck B enters at `b_start` =
  last 4-bar boundary that leaves `blend_bars` before A ends.
- Gains (staggered, gain-matched): B enters at `-21+trim` and reaches `trim` (full) BY the
  swap beat; A holds `0` until the swap, then falls to `-60` by bEnd. trim = rmsA - rmsB.
- Bass swap at the blend midpoint (bar-snapped): B's knob `0.62 → 0` in a 0.25-beat step;
  A's knob `0 → 0.5` right after. The low end changes hands in one beat — that is the
  moment the crowd feels.

## The echo-out recipe (what dj_set writes for >16% hops)
- Outgoing deck: clip `lengthBeats` = its last whole 16-beat phrase; fx chain gains
  `fx_delay {time: dotted-8th ms at its OWN bpm, feedback 0.6, mix 0}`; automation
  `fx[1].mix` rises 0 → 0.55 over the final 8 beats. Gain stays FULL (see the law).
- Incoming deck: starts AT the cut beat at full (trimmed) gain — no fade-in, no filter
  ride. DJs slam it; hesitation reads as an error.
- tempoMap steps to the incoming bpm at the cut bar. The delay tail is time-domain fx
  (ms, not beats) so it rings at the OLD groove's dotted-8th over the new tempo — that
  overhang is the classic sound of the move, not a bug.

## Reading the energy (choosing the swap bar)
- Swap INTO a downbeat that starts a phrase of deck B (its intro bars usually work:
  offsetSec 0 is fine for v1; for a mid-song entry set the clip offsetSec so `b_start`
  lands on a phrase start of B).
- Long blend (8-16 bars): melodic/progressive material, compatible keys.
- Short blend (4 bars): clashing keys or vocal-heavy decks. >16% tempo distance: echo-out.
- Keys a fifth apart or relative major/minor blend clean; a semitone apart clashes —
  shorten the blend or filter one deck hard (knob ±0.7) throughout.

## Set building
- `dj_set` is the native move (no intermediate renders): each transition steps the
  tempoMap to the incoming deck's natural bpm, so every deck plays at (or within
  warp-transparency of) its own tempo in its solo region. Style per hop is automatic;
  the printed plan marks each entry `[blend]` or `[echo_out]`.
- Tempo arcs beyond DJing: tempoMap works on ANY song — halftime drops
  ({"bar": 32, "bpm": 70}), ritardando outros (curve 0 = linear ramp), tempo switches
  between sections. Warped audio, notes, automation and lyrics all follow.
- Verify every transition numerically before trusting your ears: render and check
  clipped_samples == 0 (fx_brickwall -1.0 is in the master by default), blends'
  LUFS must not dip >2 dB below surroundings, and an echo-out's outgoing STEM must
  still carry energy right after the cut (the tail) that decays over 2-4s.
